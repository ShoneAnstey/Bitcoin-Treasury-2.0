# Expanded Addendum C — CNav (Credit NAV)

## Introduction and Definition
CNav (Credit NAV) measures yield from Bitcoin-native credit, including BTC-backed loans, structured credit, and synthetic hedges. Involves lending BTC or using it as collateral for predictable interest.

## Benefits and Objectives
- **Benefits**: Stable yields (2-18% in 2025); low volatility; secured by over-collateralization.
- **Objectives**: Earn bond-like returns; manage counterparty risk via LTV; diversify treasury income.

## Expanded Example Company: Satoshi Credit Corp.
Satoshi Credit Corp. (OTC: SATC) holds 200 BTC (~$24.8M), 10M shares, no debt. Lends 50 BTC at 8% APR, 50% LTV, provisioning 1 BTC for defaults.
- Annual gross: 4 BTC; net 3 BTC.
- Aligns with platforms offering 10.4-12.95% at 50% LTV.

## Mathematical Framework
Assumptions: APR 8%; LTV 50%; provisions 2%; defaults 1%.

1. **Loan-to-Value (LTV)**: Collateral safety.  
   Formula: Loan / Collateral.  
   Calculation: 25 / 50 = 0.50 (50%).

2. **Gross Yield**: Interest earned.  
   Formula: Principal × APR.  
   Calculation: 50 × 0.08 = 4 BTC.

3. **Yield Coverage**: Yield vs. service.  
   Formula: Gross / Debt Service (assume 1x for simplicity) = 4 / 4 = 1x.

4. **Net Yield**: After losses.  
   Formula: Gross - Provisions - (Defaults × Portfolio).  
   Calculation: 4 - 1 - (0.01 × 50) = 2.5 BTC (adjusted for example).

5. **CNav Value**: Principal + Net.  
   Calculation: 50 + 2.5 = 52.5 BTC.

## Key Metrics
| Metric | Description | Formula/Example Calculation | Example Value |
|--------|-------------|-----------------------------|---------------|
| **Loan-to-Value (LTV)** | Collateral / Loan | Loan / Collateral × 100 | 50% |
| **Gross Yield** | Annual interest | Principal × APR | 8% |
| **Yield Coverage** | Yield / Service | Gross / Service | 1.2x |
| **Credit Loss Provision** | Reserved % | Expected Losses × Size | 2% |
| **Default Rate** | Arrears % | Losses / Issued | 1% |
| **Net Yield** | After provisions | Gross × (1 - Losses) | 6% |

## Risks
- **Defaults**: Higher in drawdowns; LTV breaches trigger liquidations.
- **Regulatory**: Varying global standards.
- **Interest Rate**: Compression from competition.

## Opportunities
- **High LTV Loans**: Up to 86% with platforms like Coinbase.
- **Reshaping Lending**: BTC collateral unlocks traditional finance.
- **Yields**: 10-13% common in 2025.

## Governance & Policy Suggestions
- LTV cap: 60%; diversify borrowers.
- Provisions: 2-5% reserves.
- Annual credit audits.
