# Bitcoin Treasury 2.0 – Strategy Brief & Metrics Standard

_An open framework for yield-enhanced Bitcoin treasuries, compounding BTC/share growth through standardized metrics._

## Overview
Bitcoin Treasury Companies 1.0 primarily accumulate BTC and measure success through BTC/share and MNAV/share.  
Bitcoin Treasury 2.0 goes further: companies actively deploy their Bitcoin holdings (Lightning, derivatives, arbitrage, credit, or other productivity strategies) to generate recurring **sats income**.  

This transforms BTC from a passive balance sheet asset into a productive one — compounding per-share growth, improving capital efficiency, and strengthening Bitcoin’s financial infrastructure.

---

## Key Benefits
- **Recurring Income Layer** – Converts BTC into a productive asset generating yield.  
- **Compounding Growth** – Reinvested yield grows BTC/share independent of price.  
- **Capital Efficiency** – Yield offsets debt service and operating costs.  
- **Valuation Uplift** – Enables hybrid asset + income multiples.  
- **Ecosystem Leadership** – Deepens Bitcoin liquidity and infrastructure strength.  

---

## Core Metrics
Treasury 2.0 builds on legacy metrics while adding yield-enhanced KPIs:

- BTC Holdings, Market Value, BTC/share, MNAV/share (baseline).  
- **BNAV/share** – Umbrella NAV that includes net yield.  
- **LNav / DNav / ANav / CNav** – Strategy-specific NAV variants.  
- **Sats per Share** – Ownership measured in satoshis.  
- **Yield Efficiency** – Sats yield per BTC and per $1M NAV.  
- **Compounded Treasury Growth (CTG)** – BTC × (1 + Yield%)^Years.  

---

## Risks
- BTC price volatility impacts NAV and yield in dollar terms.  
- Yield compression as competition grows.  
- Operational and custody failures.  
- Regulatory and accounting uncertainty.  

---

## Opportunities
- Valuation uplift from hybrid asset + income model.  
- Sats-based dividends or buybacks.  
- Yield-backed ETFs, bonds, and structured products.  
- Data advantage via liquidity telemetry.
