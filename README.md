## 📖 Overview
This repository contains the **Bitcoin Treasury 2.0 – Strategy Brief & Metrics Standard**, a comprehensive framework for defining, measuring, and reporting yield-enhanced Bitcoin treasuries.  

Bitcoin Treasury 1.0 companies primarily accumulated BTC and reported value via BTC/share and MNAV/share.  
**Bitcoin Treasury 2.0** goes further — companies actively deploy their Bitcoin holdings for yield (Lightning, derivatives, arbitrage, credit strategies) to generate recurring sats income, compounding treasury growth while deepening Bitcoin’s financial infrastructure.

## 📝 Key Highlights

### Core Concepts
- **Treasury 1.0**: Passive BTC accumulation.  
- **Treasury 2.0**: Active yield generation + sats productivity reporting.  
- **BNAV/share**: Expanded NAV that incorporates yield-enhanced strategies.  

### NAV Variants
- **LNav** – Lightning yield (routing, channel leasing, swaps).  
- **DNav** – Derivatives yield (covered calls, futures basis, funding).  
- **ANav** – Arbitrage yield (cross-exchange, orderbook liquidity).  
- **CNav** – Credit yield (BTC-backed lending, structured credit).  

### Scoreboard Metrics
- BTC/share, MNAV/share, BNAV/share.  
- Yield, deployment efficiency, sats productivity.  
- Risk & stability ratios, benchmarks vs T-bills and DeFi.

## 🚀 Opportunities
- **Recurring sats income** – transforming BTC from a passive asset into a productive treasury.  
- **Compounded growth** – yield reinvestment grows BTC/share independent of price.  
- **Investor appeal** – enables REIT- or infra-style valuation multiples.  
- **Global adoption** – especially compelling where ETFs or bond markets are limited.
